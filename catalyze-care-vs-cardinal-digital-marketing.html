Catalyze Care vs Cardinal Digital Marketing

Updated October 1, 2026 · 7 min read·By Andrew Brazis

Cardinal Digital Marketing and Catalyze Care are both healthcare-only marketing agencies, built for different buyers. Cardinal describes itself as a healthcare performance marketing agency for provider groups ready to scale fast, with paid media, SEO, website design, and media mix modeling. Catalyze Care works with therapy practices, psychiatry clinics, and multi-location specialty groups. We start with the doctors who already refer in your market, then add SEO and paid ads at published prices: referral marketing $5,000/month (3-month minimum), SEO $799/month, and paid ads $2,500/month plus media.

In one sentenceChoose Cardinal for a large paid media and measurement program across many locations; choose Catalyze Care when referring doctors drive your growth and you want fees you can read before the first call.

What Cardinal Digital Marketing focuses on

Cardinal's public site describes performance marketing for high-growth healthcare groups: Google, Meta, TikTok, and display advertising, SEO, website design, conversion rate work, and business intelligence by service line and location. Its RevRx planning tool uses media mix modeling to guide where budget goes. The groups it lists include behavioral health providers, hospitals and health systems, multi-location provider groups, addiction treatment centers, dental groups and DSOs, senior care, urgent care, and private equity portfolio companies. Since January 2026, Cardinal has operated as the healthcare division of Power Digital Marketing.

Sources: Cardinal's home, services, specialties, and Power Digital partnership pages, reviewed October 1, 2026. Agencies update their services often, so check their current pages before you decide.

What Catalyze Care focuses on

  • Referral marketing, $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum. We find the offices that already send patients in your specialty and reach them by fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail.
  • SEO, $799/month. Your Google Business Profile, directory listings, service and location pages, and technical fixes, so patients find you when they search.
  • Paid ads, $2,500/month management, media billed separately. Google and Meta campaigns aimed at booked appointments, with tracking that keeps patient health information out of ad platforms.
  • Monthly reports with booked-inquiry fields your intake team can check.

When Cardinal is the better fit

Cardinal is a strong conversation when you run many locations, spend heavily on paid media, and want forecasting and media mix modeling to decide where each dollar goes. Health systems, DSOs, and private equity groups adding sites fit that description well.

When Catalyze Care is the better fit

Catalyze Care fits when new patients mostly arrive through other clinicians, as they do for many therapy and psychiatry practices, and you want that outreach run alongside SEO and paid ads on one plan. It also fits when you want to know the fee before the first call. Start with our healthcare marketing agency overview, or see how healthcare referral marketing works.

How to compare the two

  1. Name the gap. Write down whether growth is limited by referrals, search visibility, or near-term demand. Our guide to choosing a healthcare marketing agency walks through it.
  2. Get fees in writing. Catalyze Care publishes $5,000 / $799 / $2,500. Ask every firm for its monthly fee and minimum term on the same page as its cancel terms.
  3. Ask what the monthly report shows. You want booked appointments by source, checked against your intake. Our guide to reading a monthly healthcare marketing report lists the fields.
  4. Send both firms the same questions. Use the healthcare marketing RFP questions so the answers line up.
