About Catalyze Care

Catalyze Care is a healthcare marketing agency for healthcare practices and provider groups. Work is referral marketing, SEO, and paid ads built on the same claims-backed referral intelligence — not a generalist stack retrofitted for healthcare.

What Catalyze Care does

  • Healthcare marketing agency — the firm-level overview of referral, SEO, and paid ads on one referral map.
  • Healthcare referral marketing — outreach to providers who already send patients in your specialty, via fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail. Flagship service: referral marketing.
  • SEO for healthcare practices — local SEO, Google Business Profile, on-page and technical work, schema, and medically reviewed content. Full engagement: SEO.
  • Paid ads for healthcare practices — HIPAA-aware Google Ads, Meta Ads, and Display aimed at booked appointments. Full engagement: paid ads.
  • Healthcare digital marketing — SEO, paid ads, and referral outreach run as one system.
