When to Pause Paid Ads vs SEO vs Referral Marketing

By Andrew Brazis · Updated September 25, 2026·10 min read

Pause the channel that is sending demand your practice cannot keep, or that is advertising a fact that is no longer true. Paid ads stop first when intake is full or a platform policy blocks the campaign. Referral outreach stops when referring offices would be promised a slot you cannot honor, or when a license or payer change makes the message wrong. SEO usually stays on unless the website would publish stale facts. Check these three facts before you pause

  1. Intake. If the front desk cannot book the next new patient in a time you will honor, pause paid first. Then pause new referral outreach. Capacity: staff intake before scaling referral or paid.
  2. Platform policy. If Google or Meta disapproves healthcare ads, pause spend until creative and landing pages match current policy. Google publishes healthcare and medicines advertising rules. Do not keep paying for disapproved traffic. Service: paid ads.
  3. License and who you take. If a clinician license, state, or payer panel changed, pause any channel whose copy still names the old footprint. That includes ads, GBP, and referral letters.
  4. Leave SEO on unless the site is wrong. Pause new SEO publishing only when the website would state facts that are no longer true. Compounding crawl work is not the same as paid spend. Service: SEO.
