When to Pause Paid Ads vs SEO vs Referral Marketing

By Andrew Brazis · Updated October 16, 2026·11 min read

Pause the channel that is sending demand your practice cannot keep, or that is advertising a fact that is no longer true. Paid ads stop first when intake is full, lead quality slips, or a platform policy blocks the campaign. Referral outreach stops when referring offices would be promised a slot you cannot honor. SEO usually stays on unless the website would publish stale facts. Catalyze Care publishes referral marketing at $5,000/month (3-month minimum), SEO at $799/month (6-month minimum), and paid ads management at $2,500/month plus media (3-month minimum).

In one sentencePause paid when intake or policy breaks. Pause referral when you cannot keep the promise to a referring office. Leave SEO on unless the site would be wrong.

Checks before you pause

Which channel to stop

  1. Intake. If the front desk cannot book the next new patient in a time you will honor, pause paid first. Then pause new referral outreach. We cover this in more detail in staff intake before scaling referral or paid.
  2. Platform policy. If Google or Meta disapproves healthcare ads, pause spend until creative and landing pages match current policy. Google publishes healthcare and medicines advertising rules. Do not keep paying for disapproved traffic. Read how we run paid ads.
  3. License and who you take. If a clinician license, state, or payer panel changed, pause any channel whose copy still names the old footprint. That includes ads, GBP, and referral letters.
  4. Leave SEO on unless the site is wrong. Pause new SEO publishing only when the website would state facts that are no longer true. Compounding crawl work is not the same as paid spend. Read how we run SEO.

Source: Google Ads, Healthcare and medicines policy.

Four checks in the report

  1. Lead quality. A call or form from someone outside your ages, payers, or service area is demand you cannot book. Pause or tighten the campaign when the inquiries stop matching who you take. Google and Meta do that job differently, which Google Ads vs Meta Ads lays out.
  2. Intake capacity. If the front desk cannot offer a time you will honor, pause paid first, then new referral outreach. The staffing check is in staff intake before scaling referral or paid.
  3. Spend versus booked inquiries. Compare the media line on the invoice with booked inquiries your front desk logged. When spend rises and booked inquiries stay flat, pause that campaign or fix the landing page before you add budget. The paid ads compliance checklist is the policy pass to run before the restart.
  4. Season. Some specialties quiet down in late December and pick up in January after deductibles reset. Plan the pause from your own prior-year schedule, and plan the restart before the busy weeks. Q4 and January demand is the planning note. A referral dip has its own checks in what to check when referral volume dips.
