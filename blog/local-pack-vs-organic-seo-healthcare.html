Local Pack vs Organic SEO for Healthcare Practices

By Andrew · Published September 12, 2026·9 min read

Local-pack SEO and organic SEO are two different Google surfaces for a healthcare practice. The local pack is the Maps-driven block of Google Business Profiles that appears for many near-me and specialty-plus-place searches. Organic SEO is the blue-link website results: service pages, crawl health, and schema that matches visible text. FAQ

What is the local pack for a healthcare practice?

The local pack is the Maps-driven block of business profiles Google shows for many near-me and specialty-plus-place searches. It is fed by Google Business Profile, categories, hours, reviews, and a consistent name, address, and phone. Guide: Google Business Profile for healthcare practices.

How is organic SEO different from the local pack?

Organic SEO is the blue-link website results: service pages, crawl health, schema that matches visible text, and medically reviewed content. A practice can appear in one surface and not the other. This page does not invent ranks.

Which surface should a practice work first?

If patients search specialty plus place and the Maps panel is incomplete, start with Google Business Profile and citations. If the query is informational or the site cannot answer who you take, start with on-page work. Checklist: local SEO checklist for healthcare practices. Service: SEO.
