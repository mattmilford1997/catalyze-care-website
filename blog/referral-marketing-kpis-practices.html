Referral Marketing KPIs Practices Should Track

By Andrew · Published September 13, 2026·8 min read

Referral marketing KPIs a practice can check against intake are outreach volume, meetings or conversations, referrals received, booked appointments, and close-the-loop latency, the time from a referred patient’s first visit to the consult note back to the referring office. The honest metric list

KPIWhat to countWhere it lives
