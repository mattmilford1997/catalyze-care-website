When Not to Hire a Healthcare Marketing Agency

By Andrew · Published September 10, 2026·8 min read

A healthcare practice should not hire a marketing agency when intake cannot take the next patient, when the only ask is a guaranteed ranking or ROI multiple, when the job is hospital leakage analytics or a logo-only project, or when the practice will not name who it takes. Those are different jobs. Do not hire when intake is the bottleneck

If the next new patient waits weeks because no one answers, authorization is stuck, or clinicians are full, buying outreach or ads adds demand the practice cannot book. Fix the calendar first. An agency that skips that question is selling a channel, not diagnosing a practice.

Do not hire for a guaranteed rank or multiple

No honest healthcare SEO engagement guarantees first-page ranks. No honest paid or referral engagement guarantees an ROI multiple. Catalyze Care’s published fees are inputs you can model—not a promised return. If a proposal leads with unnamed “300% ROI” slides, walk away. Compare published fees and reporting you can check against intake instead.

Do not hire for the wrong job

  • Hospital leakage analytics and health-system referral dashboards are a different product than outreach to referring offices.
  • Logo, brand, or website-only projects are design work. Ask a studio, not a patient-growth retainer.
  • Physician media inventory (HCP ad networks) is not claims-backed referral outreach.
  • A liaison you will not manage is a staffing decision. Contrast: physician liaison vs referral agency and agency vs in-house.

Do not hire if you will not name who you take

Kickoff needs ages, payers, services, locations, and what “booked” means. If leadership will not write that down, copy and targeting become guesswork. Catalyze Care will not invent a specialty spoke to fill the blank.

When hiring can make sense

Intake can take the next appropriate patient. You will name who you take. You want a documented channel order (referral vs SEO vs paid) and a monthly report you can check. You accept published minimums: referral is a 3-month engagement at $5,000/month because a single month is not a test. SEO at $799/month compounds over months. Paid ads at $2,500/month management plus media follow current platform healthcare policies.
