What a Healthcare-Only Agency Should Mean

By Andrew · Published September 11, 2026·8 min read

A healthcare-only marketing agency should mean the firm’s day-to-day work is healthcare practices: HIPAA-aware tracking, Google Ads healthcare policy, Google Business Profile rules, and referral outreach to other providers, not a generalist local-SEO shop with a healthcare landing page bolted on. Catalyze Care publishes fees on this site: referral marketing $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum, SEO $799/month, paid ads $2,500/month management (media separate). Healthcare-only is a scope claim, not a rank promise and not a reason to invent case studies. What healthcare-only should include

  • HIPAA-aware tracking. No diagnosis-level pixels or URL parameters. Conversion events the front desk can confirm. HHS OCR has published tracking-technology guidance. This page is not legal advice.
  • Google Ads healthcare policy. Personalized-health language, restricted drug terms, and categories that need certification. A generalist PPC template will trip those.
  • Google Business Profile and NAP. One staffed door, one profile, one honest name/address/phone string. No fake map pins.
  • Referral outreach that is not a rented doctor list. Claims-backed targeting and a report the practice can check. Playbook: what is healthcare referral marketing.
  • Published fees. Catalyze Care: $5,000 referral (3-month minimum), $799 SEO, $2,500 paid ads management (media separate).

Source: HHS OCR, Use of Online Tracking Technologies. Source: Google Ads, Healthcare and medicines policy.

What a generalist retrofit usually looks like

  • A “we also do dentists” slide on a restaurant-and-HVAC roster.
  • Condition remarketing or Customer Match built from clinical page visits.
  • One Google Business Profile for every city in the service area, including cities with no office.
  • Unnamed ROI multiples in place of published retainers.
  • Agency-owned ad accounts you never get admin on.

Day-to-day work is healthcare practices: HIPAA-aware tracking, Google Ads healthcare policy, Google Business Profile rules, and referral outreach to other providers. A homepage badge on a generalist local-SEO shop is not the same thing.

When should a practice not hire even a healthcare-only firm?
