What “Healthcare-Only Agency” Should Mean

By Andrew · Published September 11, 2026·9 min read

“Healthcare-only agency” should mean the firm’s published work is patient-growth for healthcare practices and provider groups—referral outreach, healthcare SEO, and HIPAA-aware paid ads—not a generalist local-SEO or PPC shop that added a medical vertical last quarter. Healthcare marketing advice can affect how patients find care (YMYL). That is a reason to refuse invented case names, leakage percentages, and ROI multiples—not a license to claim a rank. Catalyze Care publishes fees on this site: referral marketing $5,000/month (3-month minimum), SEO $799/month, paid ads $2,500/month management (media separate). Definition: what is a healthcare marketing agency. When not to hire: when not to hire a healthcare marketing agency. Firm overview: healthcare marketing agency.

In one sentenceHealthcare-only is a constraint you can inspect—published healthcare work, HIPAA-aware tracking, and no invented ROI—not a badge that guarantees results.

This is a YMYL page

Advice about how a practice markets care can change who finds an appointment. Google’s public guidance still starts with helpful, people-first pages. Healthcare-only vs a generalist retrofit

AskHealthcare-only should answerGeneralist retrofit often answers
Who is the client?Practices and provider groups“We also do dentists and lawyers”
What channels?Referral, SEO, and/or paid with healthcare policyThe same local-SEO package as last quarter
TrackingBooked inquiries; no PHI in pixelsThe same remarketing pixel as e-commerce
ProofNamed work with permission; published feesUnnamed “4x ROI” slides
What they will not doHospital leakage dashboards, condition targeting, invented ranksWhatever the RFP listed

A generalist can still be competent. The retrofit problem is copying a restaurant or HVAC playbook onto a practice: condition targeting, clinical-page remarketing, or a guaranteed first-page clause. Those are the wrong tools. HIPAA limits marketing uses of PHI; Google’s healthcare and medicines policy limits claims and targeting. Neither is optional copy.

Source: HHS, HIPAA and marketing. Source: Google Ads, Healthcare and medicines policy.
