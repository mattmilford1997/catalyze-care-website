How Practices Should Evaluate Referral Marketing Vendors

By Andrew · Published September 12, 2026·9 min read

A referral marketing vendor is evaluable when three lines sit in the same packet as the fee: where the target list comes from, which close-the-loop fields the monthly report will show, and how outreach stays inside compliance (no payment for referrals, PHI stays in the practice's systems). Catalyze Care publishes referral marketing at $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum and does not invent a leakage percentage, an unnamed case result, or a guaranteed patient count per fax. Pair this page with the referral marketing playbook, the referral marketing service page, and close-the-loop referral reporting.

In one sentenceIf the vendor cannot name the data source, the monthly fields, and the compliance line, do not sign yet.

Six checks, in order

  1. Ask where the list comes from. Claims-backed referral patterns show who already sends this specialty. A rented doctor directory shows who exists. Those are different products. Catalyze Care campaigns use claims-backed referral intelligence covering 48 million+ relationships across 120+ specialties. Playbook: what is healthcare referral marketing.
  2. Confirm the practice owns the list. Target list, suppression list, and approved fax or email copy should sit under the practice. The vendor works as a user. Ask for the export format in the SOW. If you leave, the list should leave with you.
  3. Read the close-the-loop fields. Offices contacted, responses, booked inquiries from your intake, and whether a consult note went back to the referring office. If a number cannot be checked against your system, it does not belong on the slide. This page does not invent a leakage percentage. Fields: close-the-loop referral reporting. Analytics: referral analytics: closing the loop.
  4. Put compliance next to the fee. Outreach should not pay for referrals. CMS and HHS OIG publish public Stark and Anti-Kickback materials. That is a high-level note, not legal advice. Ask how PHI stays in the practice's systems and who approves copy before a fax goes out.
  5. Match the fee to a published number. Catalyze Care publishes $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum because a single month is not a test. Service and intake: referral marketing. Lander: healthcare referral marketing.
  6. Write the pause conditions. Intake full, a license lapse, or a list the practice will not name should stop outreach. Pause is not a promised result. When not to hire: when not to hire a healthcare marketing agency.

Vendor red flags

  • The list is “proprietary” and will not export.
  • The report is a vanity dashboard with no booked-inquiry field from your system.
  • The deck leads with an unnamed leakage percentage or a guaranteed patient count.
  • Copy goes out without a practice-approved ask.
  • Cancel terms live in a later legal packet, not next to the $5,000 line.
