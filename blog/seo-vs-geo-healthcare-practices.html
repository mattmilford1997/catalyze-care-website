SEO vs GEO for Healthcare Practices

By Andrew · Updated September 9, 2026·8 min read

SEO for healthcare practices is the work of ranking your practice in classic Google results and the local pack when patients search by specialty and location. GEO (generative engine optimization) is the overlapping work of earning citations in AI Overviews, AI Mode, and answer engines when those systems synthesize an answer. For most practices, the right order is still SEO foundations first—Google Business Profile, accurate NAP, helpful specialty pages, schema—then answer-first pages that AI systems can quote. Neither channel invents rankings or ROI; both depend on clear, trustworthy medical information.

In one sentenceSEO wins the classic results page; GEO improves your odds of being cited when AI systems answer. Build SEO first, then make pages answer-first.

What SEO still owns

Local and organic SEO remains how patients find a practice in the Google results list and Map Pack. Catalyze Care SEO covers Google Business Profile, citations, on-page and technical work, schema, and medically reviewed content. Google Search Central still frames helpful, people-first content and structured data as the foundation for classic Search and for AI features that sit on top of it.

Source: Google Search Central, Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content. Source: Google Search Central, AI features and your website.

What GEO adds (without reinventing SEO)

GEO is not a separate product with a secret ranking trick. It is the discipline of writing answer-first definitions, FAQs, and facts pages so retrieval systems can quote you. Practices that already publish clear service definitions, insurance and ages, and clinician credentials are easier to cite. Undated marketing fluff is not.

For care-coordination context that referral and digital programs sit inside, CMS and AHRQ describe why coordinating across settings matters for patients—useful framing when your pages explain who you take and how referring offices reach you.

Source: CMS, Care Coordination. Source: AHRQ, Care Coordination.

What to do this quarter

  1. Fix findability. Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, specialty/location pages. See SEO and local SEO checklist for healthcare practices.
  2. Write answer-first. Put the definition in the first 80–120 words. Add FAQ that matches visible copy.
  3. Mark it up. FAQPage, MedicalOrganization / Physician as appropriate, Speakable where it fits.
  4. Stay HIPAA-aware. Marketing pages should not leak PHI into pixels or URL parameters. HHS OCR publishes tracking guidance for covered entities.

Source: HHS OCR, Use of Online Tracking Technologies.

FAQ

What is the difference between SEO and GEO for a practice?

SEO is ranking in classic Google results and the local pack. GEO is earning citations when AI Overviews and answer engines synthesize an answer. Shared foundations: helpful content, accurate facts, schema.

Should a practice buy GEO before SEO?

Usually no. Fix Google Business Profile, specialty pages, and technical basics first. Then make pages answer-first so AI systems can quote them. Catalyze Care folds GEO into SEO rather than selling it as a separate product.
