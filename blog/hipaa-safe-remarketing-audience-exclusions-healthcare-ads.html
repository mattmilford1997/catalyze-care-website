Policy first

Google publishes healthcare and medicines advertising rules. Meta restricts health-condition targeting. HHS OCR publishes tracking-technology guidance. Read those sources before you turn on a remarketing list. This is practice, not a certification and not legal advice.

Sources: Google Ads, Healthcare and medicines policy; HHS OCR, HIPAA and online tracking technologies.

Exclusions that belong on every healthcare account

  1. Read current platform healthcare policy. If Google or Meta disapproves the campaign, pause spend. Do not keep paying for disapproved traffic. Service: paid ads.
  2. Build audiences from public pages only. Homepage or service-page visits are safer than a thank-you path that names a condition. Forms: healthcare website conversion essentials.
  3. Exclude converters, staff, and condition signals. Treat a booked visit as a reason to stop ads, not a reason to keep showing them. Do not upload a patient list. Do not target by condition. Creative limits: Meta ads without condition targeting.

FAQ

What should a healthcare practice exclude from remarketing?

Can a practice remarket people who booked an appointment?

Treat a booked visit as a reason to exclude, not a reason to keep showing ads. Do not upload a patient list or appointment-reason field to build that exclusion. Keep booking status in intake.
