Agency vs In-House Healthcare Marketing

By Andrew · Updated September 9, 2026·8 min read

Choose an in-house marketer when you have enough continuous work, clinical review capacity, and management time to run referral outreach, SEO, and paid ads week after week. Choose a healthcare marketing agency when you need specialized channel execution—claims-backed referral targeting, HIPAA-aware ads, local SEO—without hiring three roles. Either path still needs a named owner at the practice for intake, referring-provider fields, and clinical review of claims; the agency does not replace those jobs. Catalyze Care publishes fees on this site: $5,000/month referral marketing (3-month minimum), $799/month SEO, and $2,500/month paid ads (media spend separate). This page does not invent ROI multiples for either path.

In one sentenceIn-house wins on day-to-day control; a healthcare agency wins on specialized channel depth—compare published fees, not invented ROI.

What “in-house” actually costs

A single generalist marketer rarely covers physician outreach, Google Ads policy for healthcare, and local SEO at once. Fully loaded cost includes salary, benefits, tools, creative, and management time. Public BLS wage data for related sales and marketing roles shows mid-five to low-six-figure base pay bands before benefits—useful as a floor, not as Catalyze pricing.

Source: U.S. BLS, Wholesale and Manufacturing Sales Representatives (May 2024 median pay context for technical/scientific sales).

What an agency should put on the page

  • Published fees. Catalyze Care: $5,000 referral (3-month minimum), $799 SEO, $2,500 paid ads.
  • Healthcare-only focus. Fax workflows, condition-targeting limits, appointment-form constraints.
  • Data ownership. You should keep lists, creatives, and ad accounts.
  • Reporting cadence. Monthly outreach, rankings, or spend—not a promised multiple.

Hybrid that usually works

Keep intake, clinical review, and brand voice in-house. Outsource the channel that needs specialized tooling: claims-backed referral lists, ad policy navigation, or technical SEO. Sequence with healthcare digital marketing rather than buying three disconnected retainers on day one.

When is in-house better than an agency?
