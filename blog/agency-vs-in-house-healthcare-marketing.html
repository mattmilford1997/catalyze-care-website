Agency vs In-House Healthcare Marketing

By Andrew Brazis · Updated September 27, 2026·7 min read

Choose in-house marketing when you have steady work for one or two channels, clinical review capacity, and a manager with time to coach. Choose a healthcare marketing agency when you need specialist work across referral outreach, SEO, or paid ads without hiring for each role. Either way, keep intake, clinical review, and referring-office follow-up inside the practice. Catalyze Care publishes its fees: referral marketing $5,000/month (3-month minimum), SEO $799/month, and paid ads management $2,500/month with media billed separately.

In one sentenceIn-house wins on day-to-day control, an agency wins on specialist channel depth, and most practices do best with a hybrid that keeps intake and clinical review inside.

What an in-house hire really costs

One generalist marketer rarely covers physician outreach, healthcare ad policy, and local SEO at the same depth. Budget for salary, benefits, software, creative, and the manager hours spent coaching. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes pay data for advertising, promotions, and marketing managers that works as a starting point for your area.

Lean in-house when

  • You need steady content and one main channel, and you can hire and manage for it.
  • Local SEO and Business Profile upkeep can be learned and done weekly by staff.
  • Brand voice and clinical review need a daily presence in the office.

Lean agency when

  • Referral outreach has to reach many offices on a steady cadence with a researched target list.
  • Google and Meta ads need someone current on healthcare policy and tracking that keeps patient data out of ad platforms.
  • You want SEO, paid ads, and referral work sharing one specialty and geography plan without three new hires.

The hybrid most practices use

Keep intake, clinical review, and brand voice in-house. Hand the channel that needs specialist tools and cadence to an agency. Sequence channels on one plan, as described in our overview of healthcare digital marketing, instead of buying three separate retainers on day one.

What to ask before you choose

Ask any agency for written fees with media separate from management, practice-owned accounts, and a sample monthly report. Our fee transparency checklist covers the line items, and what the first 30 days should look like shows what to expect after you sign.

FAQ

When is in-house marketing better than an agency?

When you have steady work for one or two channels, clinical review capacity, and a manager who can coach the marketer every week.

When is a healthcare marketing agency the better fit?

When you need referral outreach, healthcare ad policy knowledge, or local SEO depth quickly, or want several channels on one plan without hiring for each role.

What should stay in-house if we hire an agency?

Intake, clinical review of any health claim, brand voice, and follow-up with referring offices. The agency runs the channel work around them.
