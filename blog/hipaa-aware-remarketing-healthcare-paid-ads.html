HIPAA-Aware Remarketing for Healthcare Paid Ads

By Andrew Brazis · Updated September 26, 2026·9 min read

HIPAA-aware remarketing for healthcare paid ads means showing follow-up ads to people who visited public pages, without building audiences from health conditions, without putting a diagnosis in the URL, and without sending PHI through the ad platform. What HIPAA-aware remarketing is (and is not)

It is a frequency-capped follow-up to visitors of public service or location pages. It is not a list of patients by diagnosis, not a pixel firing from an authenticated chart, and not an ad that names a condition in the headline. Platform policy and counsel rules come first. For Meta creative, read HIPAA-aware Meta ads creative and landing pages. For Meta audiences, read Meta ads for healthcare without condition targeting.

Audience and URL rules

  • Public pages only. Build remarketing lists from marketing site URLs that do not encode a diagnosis.
  • Generic thank-you path. Use /thank-you or /thanks. Never /thank-you-diabetes or similar.
  • No PHI in query strings. Appointment confirmation pages must not pass names, MRNs, or condition codes into ad tags.
  • Exclude converters you can identify without PHI. Staff IPs, vendor traffic, and completed-form audiences built from non-PHI events. For a step-by-step guide, read audience exclusions.

Creative and landing path

  1. Approved claims only. Same counsel-approved lines as the search ads.
  2. Phone CTA on mobile. Visible above the fold on the remarketing landing page.
  3. Intake-ready form. Fields front desk can process the same day.
  4. Match the click path. Remarketing should land on a focused page, not a homepage maze. For the full checklist, read paid search landing page checklist.

How to turn on HIPAA-aware remarketing

  1. Audit URLs and tags. Remove condition strings and PHI query parameters from pages that fire ad tags.
  2. Build public-page audiences. Service and location pages only. Cap frequency.
  3. Apply exclusions. Converters, staff, and vendors without using health signals.
  4. Ship approved creative to a checklist landing page. Phone CTA, clean thank-you URL, intake-ready form.
