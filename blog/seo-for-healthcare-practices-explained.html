SEO for Healthcare Practices, Explained

By Andrew · Updated September 1, 2026·4 min read

SEO for healthcare practices is the work of ranking a practice in Google when patients search by specialty and location. Catalyze Care SEO covers Google Business Profile, local citations, on-page and technical SEO, schema markup, and medically reviewed content. Work is tailored by specialty and geography.

That is the definition on SEO for healthcare practices. The full engagement — what is included each month, and the audit form — is on the SEO service page.

What patients actually search

Patients type specialty plus place: “child psychiatrist Denver Medicaid,” “speech therapist near me,” “ABA therapy Richmond.” A practice that does not appear for those queries is invisible at the moment of intent. Local pack, Maps, and organic results all matter. For multi-state telehealth, state and city pages do the same job without a waiting room.

What the work includes

  • Google Business Profile — name, category, hours, phone, services, and reviews that match how the practice actually operates.
  • Citations and directories — consistent name, address, phone, and insurance facts across healthcare directories.
  • On-page and technical SEO — service pages that answer the query, schema markup, and a site that loads and indexes cleanly.
  • Medically reviewed content — pages written for patients and referring offices, not keyword filler.

How SEO connects to the rest of the stack

SEO captures patients who are already searching. Referral marketing reaches the offices that send patients before they search. Paid ads buy nearer-term demand while pages accrue. Catalyze Care runs those on the same specialty and geography so reporting is not three unrelated dashboards.
