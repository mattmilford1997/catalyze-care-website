Paid Search Landing Page Checklist for Healthcare

By Andrew Brazis · Updated September 26, 2026·9 min read

A healthcare paid search landing page is ready when claims match what the practice can say, the phone CTA is visible on a phone screen, and the form only asks for fields intake can process without diagnosis-level capture on the thank-you URL. Why the landing page is part of the ad account

Policy review looks at the destination, not just the headline. A clean keyword list still fails when the page over-promises outcomes, hides the phone, or dumps PHI into a thank-you URL. Checklist before you spend

CheckPass looks like
Claims and offersOnly statements counsel and the practice approve. No outcome promises the practice will not honor.
Phone CTATap-to-call visible without scrolling on a phone. Matches the GBP and site footer.
Form fieldsName, phone, reason-for-visit at a high level. No diagnosis checklist that paints the thank-you URL.
TrackingGeneric thank-you page. No condition in the URL path. For pixel rules, read HIPAA marketing pixels and tracking.
RemarketingPatient and convert audiences excluded where the product allows. For a step-by-step guide, read HIPAA-safe remarketing.
Mobile layoutReadable type, no horizontal scroll, form usable with one thumb.

For Meta creative, read HIPAA-aware Meta ads creative and landing pages. If your services need certification, read Google Ads healthcare and LegitScript.

What not to put on the page

  • Before/after claims the practice cannot substantiate.
  • Condition-named thank-you URLs or pixels.
  • Hidden fees or insurance language the front desk will not honor.
  • A CPC or return figure Catalyze Care does not publish.

Multi-location note

Each ad group should land on a page that matches the location's phone and hours when locations differ. For campaign layout, read multi-location healthcare paid ads structure.

How to clear a landing page in one pass

  1. Counsel and clinical review. Mark every claim. Remove anything that cannot ship.
  2. Phone and form. Tap-to-call above the fold. Form fields intake already uses.
  3. Tracking strip. Generic thank-you. No diagnosis in URLs. For the website side, read HIPAA-aware Google Ads conversion tracking.
  4. Audience exclusions. Apply patient and convert exclusions before launch.
