Review Generation and Reputation for Healthcare Practices

By Andrew Brazis · Updated September 27, 2026·7 min read

Review generation for a healthcare practice means asking patients who completed a visit to leave an honest review on the profiles you manage, mainly Google Business Profile, and replying in a way that protects their privacy. Accurate names, hours, and phone numbers come first, because reviews on a profile with the wrong details send patients to the wrong place. In one sentenceKeep profiles accurate, ask every patient after a completed visit with a direct link, and reply to every review without confirming that the reviewer is a patient.

Why honest reviews matter more in healthcare

Reviews sit next to decisions about someone's health, and platforms and regulators treat them seriously. Google's prohibited and restricted content policy covers fake and incentivized reviews, and the FTC's 2024 rule on fake reviews and testimonials covers buying them. A steady ask after real visits is the safe path and the one that lasts.

A review workflow your front desk can run

  1. Confirm one profile per staffed location. Each office has its own verified Google Business Profile.
  2. Fix details first. Name, phone, hours, and website link match your site and major directories.
  3. Ask after the visit. Send a short text or email with a direct link to that location's review form.
  4. Reply within a few days. Thank the reviewer and keep every reply free of clinical or visit details.
  5. Review the month. Look at new reviews by location and recurring themes to share with staff.

Groups with several offices should send each patient to the profile for the office they visited. Our guide to multi-location review routing shows how.

How to reply without exposing patient information

A reply that confirms someone was seen, mentions a treatment, or discusses their visit discloses health information. Keep replies general: thank the person, state your commitment to care, and invite them to call the office manager directly. Have your privacy officer approve a few reply templates your team can reuse.

Showing ratings on your website

If you show a star rating on your site, pull it from a live platform count you can point to, and keep it current. Testimonials on the site should come from patients who gave written permission.

FAQ

Can a practice offer incentives for reviews?

Google restricts incentivized reviews, and the FTC's 2024 rule covers buying them. Ask every patient for honest feedback after a completed visit and check current policies with counsel.

How should we reply to a negative review?

Thank the reviewer, keep the reply free of any visit or clinical details, and invite them to contact the office manager directly. Move the conversation offline.

How do multi-location practices route reviews?

Send each patient the review link for the location they visited, so reviews build on the correct Google Business Profile.
