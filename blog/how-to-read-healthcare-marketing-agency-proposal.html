How to Read a Healthcare Marketing Agency Proposal

By Andrew · Published September 11, 2026·9 min read

A healthcare marketing agency proposal should state four things in the same document as the fee: who owns accounts and lists, how HIPAA-aware tracking is set up, what the monthly report contains, and how cancel or pause works. Catalyze Care publishes fees on this site: referral marketing $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum, SEO $799/month, and paid ads $2,500/month management (media separate). A proposal that leads with unnamed ROI multiples, keeps logins at the agency, or skips cancel terms is not ready to sign. Pair this with how to choose a healthcare marketing agency, questions before signing a healthcare marketing contract, and how Catalyze Care works.

In one sentenceIf fees, data ownership, reporting you can check, and cancel terms are not in the same packet, do not sign yet.

Read the proposal as a contract, not a pitch deck

Six steps to read the packet

  1. Find the fee and what is extra. Write the retainer, the minimum, and what is billed separately (media, directories, print). Catalyze Care’s published numbers are $5,000 referral (3-month minimum), $799 SEO, and $2,500 paid ads management with media separate. This page does not invent a performance SLA.
  2. Confirm who owns accounts and lists. Google Ads, Meta, GBP, Analytics, Search Console, and the CMS should sit under the practice. The agency works as a user. Ask who owns the referral target list, suppression list, and approved copy if you leave.
  3. Read the HIPAA-aware tracking line. Conversion events should measure booked inquiries the front desk can confirm. Do not put diagnosis-level values in pixels or URL parameters. HHS OCR has published tracking-technology guidance for covered entities. This page is not legal advice.
  4. Match the monthly report to intake. Activity plus booked-inquiry fields you already own. If a number cannot be checked against your system, it does not belong on the slide. Process: how Catalyze Care works.
  5. Find cancel and pause terms. Referral marketing has a published 3-month minimum because a single month is not a test. SEO and paid ads are monthly retainers at the published fees. Ask for notice period, what happens to logins on the last day, and when work pauses (intake full, license lapse, policy block).
  6. Reject invented proof. Walk away from unnamed ROI multiples, fake case-study names, or a guaranteed rank. Compare published fees and reporting you can check instead.

What this page will not promise

  • No invented response-time SLAs or “first-page in 90 days” clauses.
  • No unnamed case-study percentages. Permissioned outcomes only: case studies.
  • No extra certifications this site does not already publish.

If the packet is a pitch without those four items, read when not to hire a healthcare marketing agency before you spend another meeting on it.

FAQ

What four things must a healthcare marketing proposal include?

Fees (and what is billed separately), who owns accounts and lists, what the monthly report lets you check against intake, and cancel or pause terms. If those are not in the same document as the price, do not sign yet.

What fees does Catalyze Care publish?

Referral marketing $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum; SEO $799/month; paid ads $2,500/month management (media spend separate). This page does not invent ROI or SLA percentages.

What is a proposal red flag?

Unnamed ROI multiples, the agency keeping admin on Google or Meta accounts, PHI in pixels or URLs, a lock-in longer than the published minimum with no pause language, or a refusal to say who owns the referral list if you leave.
