Call Tracking vs EHR and Practice-Management Attribution

By Andrew Brazis · Updated September 20, 2026·10 min read

Call tracking can prove which published number was dialed and which campaign owned that number. An EHR or practice-management system can prove whether a new patient was booked and seen. Neither should send diagnosis-level data to an ad platform. Catalyze Care reports booked-inquiry fields from your intake, not a vendor-forecasted patient count from call duration. What each system can prove

SystemWhat it can proveWhat it cannot prove
Call trackingWhich number was dialed, which campaign or landing page owned that number, call lengthA diagnosis, a booked visit, or that the caller became a patient
EHR / practice managementWhether a new patient was booked and seen, payer, location, clinicianWhich ad click caused the visit, unless intake recorded a source the practice already owns
Ad platform pixelsA public-page event you chose to fireA condition, appointment reason, or chart note. Do not send those fields.

PHI-safe split

  1. Keep PHI in the practice system. Diagnosis, appointment reason, and chart notes stay in the EHR or practice-management system. HHS OCR publishes tracking-technology guidance. Not legal advice.
  2. Use call tracking for campaign-to-phone only. A tracking number can show which campaign owned the dial. It does not show a booked visit. Paid work: paid ads.
  3. Reconcile booked inquiries in intake. Score the month on fields the front desk already records. Do not ask a vendor to forecast a patient count from call duration. Stack: healthcare marketing stack map.
  4. Keep thank-you URLs and pixels clean. Do not put a condition in a thank-you URL or fire a pixel that names the service as a diagnosis. Conversion path: healthcare website conversion essentials. Tracking: HIPAA-aware Google Ads conversion tracking.

FAQ

What can call tracking prove for a healthcare practice?

Call tracking can prove which published number was dialed, which campaign or landing page owned that number, and how long the call lasted. It cannot prove a diagnosis, a booked visit, or that the caller became a patient.

What can an EHR or practice-management system prove?

The EHR or practice-management system can prove whether a new patient was booked and seen. Keep those fields inside the practice. Do not send appointment reason or condition into an ad pixel.

How do you attribute marketing without sending PHI to ad platforms?

Use UTM hygiene on public URLs, call tracking for campaign-to-phone only, and booked-inquiry fields from intake. HHS OCR publishes tracking-technology guidance. Not legal advice. Longer read: healthcare marketing attribution without PHI.
