How to Brief a Healthcare Marketing Agency

By Andrew · Published September 13, 2026·9 min read

A healthcare marketing agency brief is the kickoff packet that names who you take (ICP), where you take them (service area), what marketing cannot say or track (compliance constraints), and which fields you will review each month (reporting cadence). Catalyze Care does not need diagnosis-level patient files to start. Published fees stay referral marketing $5,000/month (3-month minimum), SEO $799/month, and paid ads $2,500/month management with media separate. Pair this page with how to choose a healthcare marketing agency and the service pages for referral marketing, SEO, and paid ads.

In one sentenceWrite ICP, service area, compliance constraints, and monthly fields before the first campaign build. Do not invent an SLA.

Five kickoff inputs, in order

  1. Name the ICP. Specialty, ages served, who you will and will not take, and insurance constraints. A slogan is not an ICP. Catalyze Care does not need diagnosis-level patient files. PHI stays in the practice’s systems.
  2. Name the service area. Offices, ZIPs, counties, or states. Do not pin a city with no office. Multi-state telehealth should say the licensed footprint. Guide: local SEO for multi-state telehealth.
  3. Write compliance constraints. No diagnosis-level pixels, no condition targeting, and no payment for referrals. HHS marketing guidance requires written authorization for most marketing uses of PHI. This is not legal advice.
  4. Name the reporting cadence. Monthly fields you can check against intake: outreach and booked inquiries for referral, GBP and page work for SEO, spend and booked inquiries for paid. That is slower than a vanity dashboard. It is not an invented SLA. Process: how Catalyze Care works.
  5. Confirm intake can take the next patient. If the schedule cannot book the next inquiry, do not staff a channel yet. When not to hire: when not to hire a healthcare marketing agency.

Match the brief to a channel

Referral first when other offices already send this specialty — referral marketing ($5,000/month, 3-month minimum). SEO first when patients search specialty plus place and the listing or site is the bottleneck — SEO ($799/month). Paid first when openings need nearer-term demand — paid ads ($2,500/month management, media separate). Channel order: how to choose a healthcare marketing agency.

What this brief will not include

  • No invented response-time SLA.
  • No promised patient count or ROI multiple.
  • No diagnosis-level files leaving the practice.
