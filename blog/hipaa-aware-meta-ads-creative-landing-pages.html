HIPAA-Aware Meta Ads Creative and Landing Pages

By Andrew · Published September 12, 2026·9 min read

HIPAA-aware Meta Ads creative is copy and imagery that invite a booked inquiry without targeting a health condition and without putting diagnosis-level values in pixels or URL parameters. The landing page should say who the practice sees, where, how to start, and which insurance the front desk can confirm. Six rules, in order

  1. Do not target by condition. Meta removed health-condition targeting. Do not rebuild it with creative that implies the viewer has a diagnosis. Geography, age bands the practice actually serves, and interest-adjacent signals that are not a diagnosis are the remaining levers.   2. Keep the ad account under the practice. The agency works as a user. Media is billed separately from the $2,500 management fee. Account structure (Google, same ownership rule): Google Ads account structure for healthcare practices.
  3. Write the landing page for a booked inquiry. Who you see, ages, location or telehealth footprint, how to start, and insurance the front desk can confirm. Do not put diagnosis-level values in the thank-you URL. Pixels: HIPAA marketing pixels and tracking.
  4. Measure booked inquiries, not clinical page views. HHS OCR has published tracking-technology guidance for covered entities. Conversion events should be something intake can confirm. This page is not legal advice. Conversion notes: HIPAA-aware Google Ads conversion tracking.

Creative that stays on the right side of the line

  • Show the practice, the setting, and a clear next step (call or form the front desk answers).
  • Say who you see in operational language (ages, location, telehealth) without implying the viewer’s diagnosis.
  • Do not use before-and-after medical claims this site cannot source.
  • Do not retarget people from a clinical results page.
