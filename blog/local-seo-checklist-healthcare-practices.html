Local SEO Checklist for Healthcare Practices

By Andrew · Updated September 9, 2026·8 min read

Local SEO for a brick-and-mortar or hybrid healthcare practice is the work of making your Google Business Profile, website, and citations agree on name, address, phone, hours, and services so patients can find and trust you in the Map Pack and local results. This checklist is for single-location and multi-location practices with real addresses. It is distinct from our guide on local SEO for multi-state telehealth, which covers virtual footprints. In one sentenceWin local SEO by making GBP, NAP, reviews, and specialty pages tell the same true story.

1. Google Business Profile (GBP)

  • Correct primary category and relevant secondary categories
  • Accurate hours, phone, appointment URL, and service list
  • Photos that match the real clinic (not stock-only)
  • Products/services and Q&A kept current
  • Regular posts only when they add facts patients need

Source: Google Business Profile Help, How to create a Business Profile.

2. NAP consistency

Name, Address, Phone must match across the website footer, GBP, and major directories. Suite numbers and tracking numbers are common failure points. Pick one public phone strategy and stick to it on citations.

3. Reviews

  • Respond to reviews professionally without revealing PHI
  • Never incentivize reviews in ways that violate Google policies
  • Route unhappy patients to intake before they post when possible

4. Citations and directories

Prioritize payer directories, specialty associations, and major health directories after GBP. Incomplete insurance fields lose both patients and referring offices. Use the directory profile audit for a 30-minute pass.

5. On-page local signals

  • Location + specialty in title and H1 where natural
  • Embedded map and parking/access facts
  • Unique content per location (no doorway duplicates)
  • Schema that matches visible text

Source: Google Search Central, Introduction to structured data.

6. Measurement

Track GBP calls, direction requests, site form submits, and booked appointments—not vanity impressions alone. For AI answer visibility layered on top of local SEO, see SEO vs GEO for healthcare practices.
