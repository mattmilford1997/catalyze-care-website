Healthcare Marketing Stack Map: What Catalyze Owns vs Your CRM, EHR, and Phone

By Andrew Brazis · Updated September 19, 2026·10 min read

A healthcare marketing stack has two owners. Catalyze Care owns outreach execution, SEO work on pages you approve, paid ads management in accounts you own, and the monthly report of verified activity. The practice owns the CRM or practice-management system, the EHR, the phone system, and clinical records. Catalyze Care does not claim a product integration with a named EHR or CRM. How to map the stack in four steps

  1. List what the agency will execute. Write outreach, SEO pages, ad accounts, and the monthly report. Catalyze Care publishes those as retainers, not as an EHR connector.
  2. List what the practice already owns. CRM or practice-management, EHR, phone, and clinical records stay at the practice.
  3. Name the booked field. Pick one intake field that means a first visit was scheduled. That field is the denominator for the monthly report. How to read it: how practices should read a monthly healthcare marketing report.
  4. Keep PHI out of marketing tags. Forms and thank-you pages should not put a diagnosis in a URL or a pixel. See healthcare website conversion essentials and healthcare marketing attribution without PHI.

What we will not claim

We will not name an EHR, CRM, or call-tracking product as a live Catalyze integration. If a future engagement needs a specific export, that is a written scope item, not a homepage feature. HHS OCR’s tracking-technology guidance is why diagnosis-level pixels stay off thank-you pages. That is practice, not a certification and not legal advice.

Source: HHS, Use of Online Tracking Technologies by HIPAA Covered Entities and Business Associates.
