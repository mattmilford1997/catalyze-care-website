What to Expect in the First 90 Days of Referral Marketing

By Andrew Brazis · October 16, 2026·9 min read

In the first 90 days of healthcare referral marketing, a practice should see a named list of offices that already refer in the specialty, a repeating outreach cadence, a close-the-loop habit with a named owner, and a monthly report tied to intake. Catalyze Care publishes referral marketing at $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum. Judge month 1 on delivery and office responses. Judge the quarter on new and repeat referring offices and booked appointments from your own intake.

In one sentenceMonth 1 builds the list, month 2 repeats the cadence, and month 3 shows whether referring offices heard back and whether intake booked the patients.

We explain how our healthcare referral marketing program runs from list to report.

Month 1: build the list and send the first wave

Month 1 is not a booked-appointment contest. It is the list and the first sends.

  • Who you take. Specialty, ages, payers, and the towns you can actually see. The practice approves that brief before anyone is contacted.
  • Which offices. Offices that already refer in the specialty, not a rented directory of every doctor in the ZIP code. How to build that list is in building a physician referral target list.
  • First wave. Fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail, matched to how that office already receives referrals. Each office should get a short note: who you take, how to refer, and how fast you will see the patient.
  • What to count. List size by segment, delivery failures, and any office that replied. Clean fax numbers and phone numbers that fail.

If the front desk cannot book the next new patient in a time you will honor, do not start the wave. That staffing check is in staff intake before scaling referral or paid.

Month 2: keep the cadence

Offices that did not respond need a second touch with a different angle, such as a specific service or a capacity update. Offices that did respond need a person, not another blast: a call, referral materials, and a direct contact.

A useful cadence is several touches across the channels the office already uses. The sequence we use is in the six-touch nurture cadence. Count responses and meetings in month 2. A single fax in month 1 is a test, not the whole quarter.

Month 3: close the loop and read the report

By month 3 the practice should be able to tell a sending office what happened. A named owner sends the referral status within seven days by fax, email, or portal. The steps are in closing the loop with referring offices in 7 days, and the monthly packet is in close-the-loop referral reporting.

Ask for these fields in the monthly report:

FieldWhere it comes from
Outreach volumeSends by channel and by segment
MeetingsCalls or visits the practice can name
Referrals receivedOffices that sent a patient, counted as offices and as referrals
Booked appointmentsThe practice's own intake log, matched to the referring office
Close-the-loop latencyDays from the visit to the status note back to the sending office

Field names are also in referral marketing KPIs practices should track. If the count of referrals drops, start with what to check when referral volume dips before you add more outreach.

Three things that stall a first quarter

  1. Slow intake. The referring office calls and nobody answers, or the patient waits three weeks. The office stops referring.
  2. No closed loop. The referring provider never hears what happened, and your practice slips out of mind.
  3. One wave and done. Offices often need more than one touch before they respond. A single fax is a test, not a campaign.

Specialty, payer mix, and how fast your intake team answers all move these fields, so read them against your own baseline.

What the quarter is for

The 3-month minimum matches the work. Month 1 is the list and the first wave. Month 2 is the cadence. Month 3 is when new and repeat referring offices, and booked appointments from intake, are fair fields to read. Catalyze Care publishes the fee at $5,000/month with that 3-month minimum.

Referral marketing tells offices who you take, how to refer, and how fast you will see the patient. It is not a payment for the referral.
