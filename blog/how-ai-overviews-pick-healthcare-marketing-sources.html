What answer engines can actually see

AI Overviews and other answer surfaces pull from pages they can crawl, understand, and trust enough to quote. Google Search Central documents how AI features in Search use content from the open web, and it still expects helpful, people-first pages. That is the same bar as classic SEO, plus a stronger preference for a direct answer near the top of the page.

Source: Google Search Central, AI features and your website.

For healthcare, YMYL standards still apply. Name the practice. Name who you take. Keep clinical claims in medically reviewed copy. Keep tracking HIPAA-aware. The split between ranking a page and being quoted in an answer is covered in SEO vs GEO for healthcare practices.

Signals that help a practice get cited

  • Answer first. The opening paragraph should tell a practice owner or a patient what they get, in sentences a person would say out loud.
  • Visible author and organization. An About page with a named operator beats an unsigned blog. Catalyze Care lists Andrew Brazis on About.
  • FAQ that matches the page. Schema should repeat questions the visitor can already read. Do not mark up answers the HTML does not show.
  • Local facts that agree. Hours, phone, services, and who you take should match Google Business Profile and the website.
  • Public sources on YMYL topics. When the topic is tracking, advertising rules, or privacy, link the HHS, Google, or FTC page that covers that rule.

None of this is a citation-rate formula. Engines change. Competitors publish. Your job is to be the clearest true page for the question.

What to publish this quarter

Start with the pages a patient or a referring coordinator already needs.

  1. A specialty plus location service page that names ages, payers, and footprint.
  2. A short FAQ on that page, written in the same words as the schema.
  3. An About or team page with a real person and a way to contact the firm.
  4. Google Business Profile hygiene and matching citations. Checklist: local SEO checklist for healthcare practices.
  5. One educational guide that teaches a buying decision, such as how to choose a firm or how channels share a map.

That mix supports both blue-link SEO and answer engines. It also supports the rest of the program on healthcare digital marketing: referral outreach still needs a site that explains who you take, and paid ads still need a landing page that matches the query.
