Healthcare Marketing Budget Allocation for Practices

By Andrew Brazis · Updated October 16, 2026·10 min read

A healthcare marketing budget is allocated when the practice names the gap first (referring offices, search visibility, or nearer-term booked demand), then staffs one channel at a published fee for the first three months. Catalyze Care publishes referral marketing at $5,000/month (3-month minimum), SEO at $799/month (6-month minimum), and paid ads management at $2,500/month plus media (3-month minimum). Referral marketing reaches offices that already send the specialty. SEO builds the listing and the pages patients find when they search. Paid ads cover nearer-term demand, with media billed separately. The field names for the monthly review are in the healthcare digital marketing metrics glossary.

In one sentenceWrite the gap, fund the matching channel at a published fee, and add a second channel only when intake can book the extra patients.

Five allocation steps, in order

  1. Name the gap, not a slogan. Growth either depends on other offices sending this specialty, on patients who already search specialty plus place, or on openings that need nearer-term demand. If intake cannot take the next patient, do not buy a channel yet. Go deeper with when not to hire a healthcare marketing agency.
  2. Write the published fee lines. Referral $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum. SEO $799/month with a 6-month minimum. Paid ads $2,500/month management plus media, with a 3-month minimum. A deck that replaces them with a single blended number hides which channel you bought.
  3. Staff one channel first. Referral first when offices already send this specialty. SEO first when the listing or site is the bottleneck. Paid first when openings need nearer-term demand while the other two compound. Before you compare firms, read how to choose a healthcare marketing agency.
  4. Add media only if paid is live. The $2,500 line is management. Media is extra. Reports show spend and booked-inquiry fields from your intake. Size media from what intake can book, not from a platform average.
  5. Review against intake. Month one is usually a build. Month two should show activity plus booked-inquiry fields the front desk already owns. Field names are in the healthcare digital marketing metrics glossary. You can see each step in how Catalyze Care works.

The first three months

Months 1 to 3 are a sequence, and the sequence changes with the gap. There is no split that fits every practice.

Month 1: fund one line and build it

Referral first means the $5,000 line buys a target list and the first outreach wave. SEO first means the $799 line buys Google Business Profile hygiene, citation cleanup, and the pages that say who you take. Paid first means the $2,500 management line plus a media amount you can pause, after an audit of the accounts you already have. Intake has to be able to book the next patient before any of those start.

Month 2: read booked inquiries, then decide on a second channel

Compare activity with the booked inquiries your front desk logged. If the first channel is producing inquiries you can take, and the schedule still has room, you can add the next line. If the front desk is already full, keep the second fee in the drawer. Referral marketing, SEO, and paid ads each keep their own report.

Month 3: keep the minimum, or pause the channel that broke

Referral and paid ads each carry a 3-month minimum, so month 3 is still inside that term. SEO carries a 6-month minimum, so month 3 is the middle of the build. Use the month to confirm the report fields and decide whether a second channel has a named job. If intake or a platform policy broke, the pause decision is in when to pause paid ads, SEO, or referral.

Frameworks that stay honest

Referral-led

Fund the $5,000 referral line for a full quarter. Do not add paid to “make month one look busy.” Close-the-loop fields belong in the same conversation as the fee. A useful next read is close-the-loop referral reporting.

Search-led

Fund the $799 SEO line when patients already type the specialty and the Maps panel or website is thin. FAQ

What fees should a practice use as budget inputs?

Catalyze Care publishes referral marketing at $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum, SEO at $799/month with a 6-month minimum, and paid ads management at $2,500/month plus media with a 3-month minimum. Budget each channel from those fees plus any media spend you approve.

When should referral, SEO, or paid ads get the first dollar?

Referral first when other offices already send this specialty. SEO first when patients search specialty plus place and the listing or site is the bottleneck. Paid ads first when openings need nearer-term demand while referral and SEO compound. Before you compare firms, read how to choose a healthcare marketing agency.

Which report fields should the budget review use?

Measure booked inquiries from the practice's own intake. The field names are in the healthcare digital marketing metrics glossary. Catalyze Care sets the mix with the practice: referral when other offices already send the specialty, SEO when search visibility is the gap, and paid ads when openings need nearer-term demand the front desk can book.

Can a practice run more than one channel?

Yes, when intake can take the next patient and each channel has a named job. Add the second channel in month two or three, after the first channel has a report you can check. Before you compare firms, read how to choose a healthcare marketing agency.
