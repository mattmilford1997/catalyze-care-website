Multi-Location vs Single-Site Healthcare SEO

By Andrew · Published September 10, 2026·8 min read

Multi-location healthcare SEO and single-site SEO share the same truth test: name, address, and phone must match the real clinic. The difference is that each staffed location should have its own Google Business Profile and its own NAP string, while a single site has one profile and one public address. In one sentenceOne staffed door, one GBP, one NAP string—repeat that for every location you actually operate.

Single-site: one story, everywhere

A single clinic still fails local SEO when the footer phone, GBP phone, and Healthgrades phone disagree, or when the suite is missing on one listing. The Google Business Profile for healthcare practices guide covers category, photos, Q&A, and measurement. Hours and services on the homepage should match GBP. Schema on the site should repeat the same visible address—not a different one invented for markup.

Multi-location: one profile per staffed place

When patients can visit a second office, that office needs its own GBP, its own location page with unique facts (parking, hours, clinicians who sit there), and citations that use that suite and phone. Do not clone a city page with the city name swapped. Do not pin a map in a city with no office. Google’s representation guidelines require an accurate listing; suspensions for fake or virtual-office pins are hard to reverse.

NAP consistency is the shared failure

Name, address (including suite), and phone must match across the website, GBP, and major directories. Suite numbers and tracking numbers are the usual breaks. Multi-location groups that reuse one tracking number on every citation make every listing look like the same office. Pick a public phone strategy per location and stick to it.

FAQ

Does each location need its own Google Business Profile?

Yes, when patients can visit a staffed location. Google’s representation guidelines require an accurate listing per location. Do not create a profile at an address where no one works.

What is NAP consistency?

Name, address (including suite), and phone matching across the website, GBP, and major directories. Multi-location groups must keep a distinct NAP string per location.

Is this the same as multi-state telehealth SEO?

No. This page assumes physical or hybrid locations. Telehealth-only or multi-state virtual models need a different playbook—see local SEO for multi-state telehealth.
