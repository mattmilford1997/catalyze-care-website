How to build the list (visible steps)

  1. Name who you take. Write specialty, ages, payers, and footprint in one paragraph the front desk would say out loud. If that paragraph is fuzzy, the list will be fuzzy.
  2. Pull claims-backed referring patterns. Start from who already sends this specialty, not a rented doctor directory. Catalyze Care uses claims-backed referral intelligence covering 48 million+ relationships across 120+ specialties.
  3. Prioritize nearby offices. Rank by specialty fit, geography, and whether the office already refers this kind of patient. A long list you never call is not a list.
  4. Reach on owned channels. Fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail, with a clear ask and no payment for referrals. High-level Stark and Anti-Kickback notes live in the playbook. That is not legal advice.
  5. Close the loop monthly. Report outreach and booked inquiries from intake. Refresh as affiliations change. Related list craft: how to build a referral target list.
