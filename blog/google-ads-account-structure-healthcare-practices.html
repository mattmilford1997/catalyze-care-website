The practice should own the account. Campaigns split by objective and geography. Ad groups stay tightly themed. Conversion URLs stay generic. Structure follows Google’s own account advice

Google Ads Help describes campaigns as the layer for budget, networks, and geography, and ad groups as the layer for a narrow theme with matching keywords and ads. Healthcare does not get a secret CPC. It gets extra policy: personalized-health language, restricted drug terms, and categories that need certification before anything serves.

Source: Google Ads Help, Organize your account with ad groups. Source: Google Ads, Healthcare and medicines policy.

Six steps to structure the account

  1. Put the account under the practice. The practice holds admin. The agency is a user, not the MCC owner who walks away with the data. Same rule as questions before signing a healthcare marketing contract.
  2. Segment campaigns by objective and geography. Separate Search from Display or Performance Max when the goal or location set differs. Do not mix unrelated service areas in one campaign. Brand vs non-brand is a business split, not a promised efficiency number.
  3. Theme ad groups tightly. Google Ads Help: pick a narrow theme per ad group and keep keywords, ads, and landing pages on that theme. A “therapy” dump of every keyword in one ad group makes policy review and relevance harder.
  4. Write ads against healthcare policy. Describe the service. Do not imply knowledge of the user’s condition. Confirm whether the category needs LegitScript or other certification before launch. Guide: Google Ads, LegitScript, and healthcare disapprovals.
  5. Attach HIPAA-aware conversion events. Measure appointment requests on generic thank-you URLs. No diagnosis, medication, or member ID in parameters. Rules: HIPAA-aware Google Ads conversion tracking. HHS OCR has published tracking-technology guidance. This page is not legal advice.
  6. Watch the policy tab after launch. Structure will not rescue a destination mismatch or a missing certification. Fix disapprovals quickly. Process: how Catalyze Care works.

FAQ

How should a healthcare practice structure a Google Ads account?

The practice owns the account. Segment campaigns by business objective and geography. Theme ad groups tightly so ads match keywords. Keep conversion events on generic appointment-request pages without PHI in URLs. Do not target by health condition.

Does account structure replace healthcare certification?

No. Some categories require Google or third-party certification before ads can run. Structure will not save an uncertified addiction-treatment or online-pharmacy campaign. See Google Ads, LegitScript, and healthcare disapprovals.

Where does HIPAA-aware conversion tracking fit?

Conversion events should measure booked inquiries the front desk can confirm. No diagnosis-level values in pixels or URL parameters. Full rules: HIPAA-aware Google Ads conversion tracking.
