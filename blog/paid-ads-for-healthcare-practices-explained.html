Paid Ads for Healthcare Practices, Explained

By Andrew · Updated September 1, 2026·4 min read

In one sentencePaid ads for healthcare practices are HIPAA-aware Google, Meta, and Display campaigns built to drive booked appointments — not vanity traffic, and not condition or diagnosis targeting.

Paid ads for healthcare practices are paid campaigns built to drive booked appointments, not vanity traffic. Catalyze Care runs HIPAA-aware Google Ads, Meta Ads, and Display for healthcare practices. Campaigns do not target by health condition or diagnosis.

Read that definition on paid ads for healthcare practices. Platforms, process, and the intake form are on the paid ads service page.

What “HIPAA-aware” means here

Tracking is set up so protected health information is not transmitted through ad platforms. Copy describes the service, not the reader’s condition. Remarketing is not built from condition-specific page visits. The team monitors platform policy changes that affect healthcare advertisers so accounts stay active.

Which platforms

  • Google Search and LSAs — high appointment intent when someone is already looking for the specialty.
  • Meta Ads — useful when there is an awareness gap; health-condition targeting is not available and should not be reconstructed.
  • Display — used where it supports the same appointment goal, not as a brand vanity buy.

Certification and policy

Some healthcare categories require third-party certification (for example LegitScript for addiction treatment and a growing set of telehealth prescribing cases) before Google will serve ads at all. General therapy or specialty clinic advertising often does not — until prescribing starts. Scope that before building the campaign. The launch path is on the paid ads page.

Paid ads fill capacity quickly. They do not replace referral marketing to offices that already send patients. Run both with the same specialty map.
