Referral Marketing

What a physician referral marketing agency does

By Andrew Brazis · Updated October 9, 2026·8 min read

A physician referral marketing agency finds the offices that already send patients in your specialty and stays in front of them until those referrals show up in your intake. Catalyze Care does that work for $5,000 a month with a 3-month minimum: a claims-backed target list, outreach by fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail, and a monthly report you can check against the front desk. The full service is healthcare referral marketing. The definition sits in what healthcare referral marketing is.

In one sentenceA physician referral marketing agency works the clinicians who already refer, while an ads shop buys clicks from patients who are searching.

The work in a month

Month one is three deliverables, in this order. First, a claims-backed list of offices that already refer in your specialty and geography. Catalyze Care builds that list from referral patterns covering 48 million+ relationships across 120+ specialties. Second, outreach on the channels those offices already use: fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail. The ask is operational: who you take, how to refer, and how fast you will see the patient. Third, a monthly report with outreach volume, meetings, referrals received, booked appointments from your intake, and close-the-loop latency.

That is the same motion on referral marketing. A liaison who already knows the market can still visit the offices that need a face. The agency covers the list that is larger than one person's week. The comparison is in physician liaison vs a referral agency.

How that differs from a patient-acquisition ads shop

An ads shop buys attention from people who type a specialty into Google or scroll a feed. That work has a real job when you have open slots and someone to answer the phone. It does not reach the pediatrician, PCP, or hospitalist who picks the next name without a search.

A referral agency starts on the other side of that handoff. The patient already has a clinical reason. The sending office can send again if the first visit goes smoothly and someone closes the loop. Catalyze Care can run paid ads later, at $2,500 a month for management with media billed separately, and SEO at $799 a month. Referral marketing stays first when other offices already send the specialty.

Catalyze Care is a healthcare marketing agency for therapy practices, psychiatry clinics, and multi-location specialty groups. Referral marketing comes first, then SEO and paid ads on the same plan, at those published prices.

What to ask before you hire

Three questions separate a referral agency from a rented doctor list with a logo on it.

  • Where does the list come from? Claims-backed patterns show who already sends the specialty. A directory shows who exists.
  • Which fields are on the monthly report? Outreach, meetings, referrals received, booked appointments from your intake, and close-the-loop latency. If a number cannot be checked at the front desk, it does not belong on the packet.
  • Who owns the list? The practice should keep the target list and the suppression list when the engagement ends.

Those questions, plus compliance notes on paying for referrals, are in how practices should evaluate referral marketing vendors. Outreach tells offices how to refer. It does not pay them for sending patients.
