WebMD Ignite-Style Referral Networks vs Practice-Led Outreach

By Andrew · Updated September 17, 2026·11 min read

WebMD Ignite-style referral network products typically serve health systems and large organizations that need visibility into referral patterns, leakage, and network performance. Practice-led referral outreach—Catalyze Care’s model—contacts the offices already sending patients in your specialty via fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail on claims-backed lists. What network-style products optimize for

Public materials for enterprise referral platforms emphasize health-system stakeholders, network integrity, and analytics around where patients go inside or outside a system. That is useful when the buyer is a hospital or large group managing leakage across employed and affiliated physicians. It is a different purchase when an independent practice needs the next referring pediatrician or PCP to know how to send a patient this week.

What practice-led outreach optimizes for

  • A named specialty and geography the practice can actually serve.
  • Claims-backed target lists of offices already sending similar patients.
  • Multi-channel cadence (fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, direct mail) with close-the-loop reporting.
  • Published retainer pricing a practice can budget: $5,000/month, 3-month minimum.
