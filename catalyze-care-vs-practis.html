When Practis (or Onspire) can be the better fit

Practis or Onspire can be a better conversation when the buyer wants a broader medical SEO or full-service health-organization program and is comfortable with that brand footprint. That is a different scope than Catalyze published practice retainers. Do not hire Catalyze as a silent substitute for a hospital AEO program without checking the job.

When Catalyze Care is the better fit

Catalyze Care is a better conversation when a practice wants published fees, healthcare-only execution, and practice-led healthcare referral marketing plus optional SEO for healthcare practices and paid ads for healthcare practices. Multi-provider listings hygiene: local SEO without duplicate GBP. Start with healthcare marketing agency if you are still defining the category.

How to compare without a fake scorecard

  1. Read published fees. Catalyze Care publishes $5,000 / $799 / $2,500. Ask Practis or Onspire to put a monthly fee in writing.
  2. Name the job. Medical SEO program, practice retainer, or referring-office outreach.
  3. Ask for report fields. Booked inquiries and listing ownership beat vanity rankings.
  4. Check HIPAA posture. How pixels, thank-you URLs, and call recordings are handled. Not legal advice.
