Cardinal Digital Marketing alternatives for healthcare practices

Updated October 1, 2026 · 6 min read·By Andrew Brazis

Practices look for Cardinal Digital Marketing alternatives when they want a healthcare-only agency with a different starting point than a large paid media program. Catalyze Care is one option. We start with the doctors who already refer in your market, then add SEO and paid ads at published prices: $5,000/month referral marketing (3-month minimum), $799/month SEO, and $2,500/month paid ads plus media. For the direct contrast, read Catalyze Care vs Cardinal Digital Marketing.

In one sentenceJudge every Cardinal alternative on the same four things: the job it does best, a written fee, reports tied to booked appointments, and tracking that keeps patient data out of ad platforms.

When to look beyond Cardinal

Cardinal publicly builds performance marketing for provider groups ready to scale fast, with paid media, SEO, website design, and media mix modeling (cardinaldigitalmarketing.com, reviewed October 1, 2026). That suits large groups with big media budgets. A single-specialty practice, or a group whose patients mostly come from other clinicians, may need a different first channel.

Catalyze Care, with published fees

Catalyze Care is a healthcare marketing agency for therapy practices, psychiatry clinics, and multi-location specialty groups.

  • Referral marketing, $5,000/month, 3-month minimum. Outreach by fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail to the offices that already send patients in your specialty.
  • SEO, $799/month. Google Business Profile, listings, service and location pages, and technical fixes.
  • Paid ads, $2,500/month management, media billed separately. Google and Meta campaigns aimed at booked appointments.

What to compare across alternatives

  • The job each firm does best. Paid media at scale, local search, websites, or outreach to referring doctors.
  • A fee in writing, with the minimum term and cancel terms on the same page.
  • Reports you can check against your intake, starting with booked appointments by source.
  • Tracking that keeps diagnosis-level details out of ad platform pixels and page URLs.
  • Account ownership. You keep the ad accounts, Business Profile, and website logins.

How to evaluate alternatives

  1. Name the job first. Decide whether the gap is paid media across many sites, local search, or more referrals from other clinicians.
  2. Ask for written fees. Catalyze Care publishes $5,000 / $799 / $2,500. Ask other firms for a monthly fee, minimum term, and cancel terms on one page.
  3. Check healthcare constraints. Condition-targeting limits, fax workflows, and tracking without patient data are daily realities in healthcare. Our primer on what a healthcare-only agency should mean covers them.
  4. Compare the reports. A useful report starts with booked appointments by source. The healthcare marketing RFP questions help you ask for it.
