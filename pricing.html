FAQ

What does Catalyze Care charge?

Referral marketing is $5,000/month with a 3-month minimum. SEO is $799/month with a 6-month minimum. Paid ads management is $2,500/month plus media spend, with a 3-month minimum.

Is media spend included in the paid ads fee?

Media spend is separate. The $2,500/month fee covers managing your Google Ads, Meta Ads, and Display campaigns, and you see spend on the monthly report.

Can I run more than one plan at once?

Yes. You can run referral marketing, SEO, and paid ads together. Each plan keeps its own fee and minimum term, and all of it shows up in one monthly report.
