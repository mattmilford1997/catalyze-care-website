WebMD Ignite publicly sells to health-system and health-plan marketing leaders. Named public products include Referral Growth, Care Finder (provider profiles and intake support), Edge Activation, Medscape media, and programmatic. The buyer is usually a hospital or system that wants consumer and HCP reach at network scale, including directory and content distribution. Public copy on their site has used guaranteed-lead language for some products. That is their model. It is not Catalyze’s model.
