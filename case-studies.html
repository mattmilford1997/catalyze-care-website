How we report results

Every client gets a monthly report built on the same fields, so you can check our work against your own front desk. The layout below lists each field and where its number comes from.

Report field What it shows Where the number comes from
Work shipped Outreach sent, pages published, listing fixes, and campaigns changed that month Our work log
Booked appointments by source New patients who scheduled, split by referral, search, and ads Your intake team
Referrals received Referrals that arrived from outreach offices, new and returning Your intake log
Close-the-loop time Days from a referral arriving to the update sent back to that office Your intake log
Spend Media spend and management fee, listed separately Ad platform invoices and our invoice
Next month What we will change and why Our plan, agreed with you

Example layout only. It shows the fields, not results from any client.
