How does healthcare referral marketing work?

We identify top referring providers in your specialty and geography using claims-backed referral intelligence, then run personalized outreach across fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail. Campaigns are optimized monthly against engagement and pipeline reporting.

?

What data does Catalyze Care use to find referring providers?

Campaigns are built on claims-backed referral intelligence covering 48 million+ relationships across 120+ specialties. The data shows who is referring patients, to whom, in what volumes, and for which specialties — not estimates or surveys.

?

Which channels do you use to reach referring offices?

Fax, email, phone, LinkedIn, and direct mail — the channels physician offices actually use. Outreach is personalized to the provider and specialty rather than a generic blast.

?

How is referral marketing different from paid ads?

Paid ads reach patients who are searching or browsing. Referral marketing reaches the providers who already send patients in your specialty, using claims-backed patterns to find those offices and a systematic outreach cadence to stay in front of them.

?

What happens after you identify the target list?

We launch multi-channel outreach, nurture referring offices over time, report monthly, and double down on the highest-performing channels. Target lists are updated from fresh claims data as the campaign runs.
